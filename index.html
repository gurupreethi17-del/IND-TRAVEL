The Challenge We Are Solving

India's Tourism Experience is Fragmented

A traveler in India currently juggles 6 to 8 disconnected apps for navigation, translations, state government websites, safety, local guides, and emergency numbers.

The Fragmented Tourist Journey

Disconnected Apps & High Friction
  • Navigation vs. Cultural Context: Google Maps shows routes, but lacks authentic historical folklore, architectural secrets, and audio guides.
  • Language & Dialect Barriers: Generic translation tools fail on nuanced regional Indian dialects, transit jargon (RAC/WL), and local idioms.
  • Local Community Exclusion: Traditional commercial OTAs capture commissions, while authentic village artisans, weavers, and heritage guides stay invisible.
  • Unpredictable Crowd Surges: Popular hotspots face severe overcrowding without predictive alerts or alternative time recommendations.

The IND Travel Ecosystem

Unified Tourism Intelligence & Trust Layer
  • One Intelligent Gateway: Fuses AI itinerary synthesis, camera heritage recognition, and audio guides into one cohesive app.
  • Multilingual India Companion: Speaks and understands 10 Indian and 3 foreign languages with voice recognition and spoken responses.
  • Grassroots Community Network: Gives direct visibility and verified demo badging to local guides, homestays, and handloom artisans.

    Forex Safety Protocol

    Authorized Currency Exchange Guidance

    IMPORTANT: IND Travel does NOT exchange currency. To protect against counterfeits and unlawful markups:

    • Always use RBI Authorized Full Fledged Money Changers (FFMC) or airport bank counters (SBI, ICICI).
    • Never exchange money with unauthorized street touts. Always request an official printed foreign exchange receipt.
    • Major Visa and Mastercard credit/debit cards are accepted across standard retail and dining outlets across India.
    Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA) Advisory

    Essential India Cultural Guide & Etiquette

    👞 Footwear & Temples

    Always take off shoes and socks before entering homes and religious sanctums. Leather products (belts, wallets) may be restricted in traditional Hindu shrines.

    📶 Tourist e-SIM & Wi-Fi

    Purchase an authorized prepaid tourist SIM card at major international airport arrivals (Jio or Airtel) by presenting your passport and visa stamp.

    💧 Water & Street Food

    Drink bottled mineral water from reputable sealed brands (Bisleri, Kinley, Himalayan) or verified RO purifiers. Enjoy freshly cooked, piping-hot dishes.
